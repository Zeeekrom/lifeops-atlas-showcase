# Showcase contribution rules

- This is a hand-curated public showcase with independent Git history.
  Never copy private production records, prompts, account identifiers or logs.
- Project-original work is all rights reserved. Retain third-party licences
  and respect statutory exceptions and GitHub's platform permissions.
- Keep terms, privacy facts and copyright footers in English and Chinese.
  Legal drafts remain marked AI-assisted and not lawyer-reviewed until reviewed.
- Changes under `demo/**` pushed to `main` automatically deploy Pages. Obtain
  the owner's approval for the concrete release before pushing or deploying.
- Fetch first, preserve existing changes, run `node --test test/*.test.mjs` and
  `node scripts/validate-public.mjs`, and review the full diff before commit.
- `deployment/robots.*.txt` files are candidates. The current project-path site
  needs hostname-root robots configuration; do not claim those rules are active
  or create a root repository/change DNS without separate approval.
- Film soundtrack provenance remains unverified. Do not claim its ownership
  or treat this notice as permission that has not been obtained.
- Never automate access to LinkedIn or its redirects. Supplied offline text
  may be analysed; provide known links for the user to visit manually.
